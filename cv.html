Summary

I am an aspiring roboticist and a thorough science enjoyer. I graduated with a BSc in Applied Physics from the University of Groningen in 2026 and am now pursuing an MSc in Mechatronics, Robotics, and Biomechanical Engineering at the Technical University of Munich. I am fluent in English, Polish, and Spanish and enjoy playing club football, bouldering, running and reading.
