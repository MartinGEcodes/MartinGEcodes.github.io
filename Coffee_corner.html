Books

Tomorrow and Tomorrow and Tomorrow

Gabrielle Zevin

A very fast read and an extreamly enjoyable one to that. While the main story revolves around three friends starting a video game company and the life stories that come with such a undertaking, the strong themes of hard work and freindship make the book special. Zevin's writing made me feel inspired to work hard and stay consistent while asking questions about personal dependance and the importance of great friendships. I would highly recomend.

7 Brief Lessons on Physics

Carlo Rovelli

A fairly short read with concise explanations of several key concepts in physics, such as heat, entropy, quantum mechanics, and the architecture of space. In the concluding chapter, Rovelli briefly mentions the order of time and the importance of the concept of heat, which I found very philosophical and intriguing. I highly recommend this to anyone, and especially to those who want to make conversation with a fellow physicist.

Think Like a Freak

Steven D. Levitt & Stephen J. Dubner

A very entertaining read with plenty of neat short stories that hilight the importance of understanding undeclared human incentives. I haven't read any other of their books (I think "Freakonomics" is the most famous one) yet still enjoyed this one on its own. Very entertaining with plenty of "huh that's prety neat" moments.
